## Overview

Data-QIMSI explores quantum-inspired modeling of social impact within complex networks, utilizing artificial LLM-based agents to simulate emotional dynamics. By integrating natural and artificial intelligence, the project investigates how emotions propagate through networks, employing a quantum-like approach to model affective states. Utilizing graph datasets and Python tools, it analyzes network properties and emotional interactions, revealing insights into emotional synchronization and heterogeneity. This research advances understanding of social phenomena in human-AI ecosystems, highlighting the potential of quantum-inspired models to capture complex emotional interactions and influence network behavior, contributing significantly to the study of distributed intelligence systems and collective emotional dynamics.

This repository contains two graphs generated for studying Quantum-inspired modeling of social impact in complex networks with artificial LLM-based agents. The DOI of the eponymous paper in Scientific Reports will be added after publication.

## Content

The Data-QIMSI project investigates quantum-inspired modeling of social impact within complex networks using artificial LLM-based agents. It comprises two primary components: graph datasets and a Python-based analytical framework. The datasets, formatted as edge lists, represent social networks, which are processed using NetworkX to construct graphs and compute network metrics. These metrics include node degrees and a unique measure called zeta, essential for understanding network dynamics. The project employs a quantum-like approach to model emotional dynamics, integrating natural and artificial intelligence agents. This framework facilitates the exploration of emotional interactions and synchronization within networks, offering insights into the influence of AI agents on social phenomena and collective emotional states in distributed intelligence systems.

To access the graphs, use the Python code attached.

## Algorithms

The project employs a quantum-inspired approach to model emotional dynamics within complex networks, integrating natural and artificial intelligence agents. Central to this is the use of a quantum-like representation of emotions, leveraging Mehrabian’s PAD model and Wundt’s three-dimensional theory, mapped onto a Bloch sphere. This framework allows for the simulation of emotional states as two-level cognitive systems, facilitating the analysis of network properties and emotional dynamics through agent-based modeling (ABM). The methodology reveals how network structures influence emotional synchronization, akin to quantum superradiance, suggesting that AI agents can mediate collective emotional dynamics, enhancing our understanding of social phenomena in human-AI ecosystems.
